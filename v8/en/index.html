Process

From application to investment in 4 steps

Every step from application to decision is clear, so you know what comes next.

How the process works
  1. 1

    Step 1: Application

    You fill in the online form; it takes about ten minutes. A deck helps, but send the form either way.

    ~10 minutes

  2. 2

    Step 2: Interview

    A thirty-minute conversation with our team about the problem and the people solving it.

    30 minutes

  3. 3

    Step 3: Investment committee

    We discuss the deck and the terms. A decision usually takes two weeks.

    usually ~2 weeks

  4. 4

    Step 4: Growth

    From day one: capital, mentorship and introductions to agencies and investors.

What to prepare

  • Startup name and short description
  • How much you need and a spending plan
  • Team and contact details
